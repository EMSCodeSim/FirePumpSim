FirePumpSim FAQ

What is FirePumpSim?

FirePumpSim is a visual fire pump operator training app. It gives firefighters short scenarios involving pump discharge pressure, hose friction loss, nozzle pressure, elevation, appliance loss, standpipes, master streams, relay pumping and related driver/operator concepts.

Is FirePumpSim free?

The app is free to download and includes starter training content. Optional in-app purchases may provide additional content as it becomes available. Your device’s app store shows the current products and prices before any purchase.

Who should use it?

FirePumpSim is designed for driver/operator candidates, working engineers, firefighters studying pump operations, academy students, instructors and company officers looking for quick training problems.

Can I try FirePumpSim without installing it?

Yes. Use the free browser version linked on this page. The iPhone and Android apps provide the best mobile experience.

Does the app replace department pump charts or SOPs?

No. FirePumpSim is for education, practice and classroom discussion. Always follow department SOPs/SOGs, apparatus pump charts, manufacturer data, incident command and certified instruction.

Where can I find full fire pump calculators?

FireOpsCalc.com provides the full web-based engineer toolkit, including pump pressure, friction loss, hydrant flow, relay pumping, nozzle reaction, water supply and pump-card tools.

How do I get help?

Visit FirePumpSim Support for contact information. You can also review the Privacy Policy.
